# How do I see my order after checking out as a guest?

When you checked out as a guest, we created a SidelineSwap account for you using your email. To view your order, track your package, or message the seller, sign in with that email. You don’t need a password.

### Sign in to see your order

1. Go to [sidelineswap.com/login](https://sidelineswap.com/login).
2. Enter the email you used at checkout.
3. We’ll email you a sign-in code. Enter it to sign in.
4. Open your purchase to see tracking, order details, and messages with the seller.

You’ll also land on the sign-in page if you tap **View Purchase** or **Contact Seller** in your order confirmation email. Use the same steps.

### Can’t find the sign-in email?

* Check your spam or promotions folder.
* Make sure you’re entering the exact email you used at checkout.
* Wait a minute or two and request a new code.

### Still need help?

[Contact support](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=360000525400) and include:

* The email you used at checkout
* Your Swap ID, which is in your order confirmation email
