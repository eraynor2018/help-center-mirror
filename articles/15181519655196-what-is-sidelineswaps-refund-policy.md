# What is SidelineSwap’s refund policy?

**Buyers:** SidelineSwap will issue a full refund if your item is never shipped, is damaged, or doesn't arrive as described. Learn more here.

**Sellers:** We collect and hold the payment once a Buyer purchases your item. You will be paid upon completion of the sale. You are not obligated to accept the return of an as advertised item. Learn more [here](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010360959-How-are-Sellers-Protected-on-SidelineSwap "https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010360959-How-are-Sellers-Protected-on-SidelineSwap").
