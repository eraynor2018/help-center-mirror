# When will I receive my refund after a dispute?

Your swap will be canceled and refunded within 36 hours of the package's arrival to the seller. You will only be refunded if they receive the item that they shipped you.

Once SidelineSwap refunds a payment, it’s immediately processed on our end. Please note that refunds can take up to 10 days to process, depending on your bank's refund protocol.
