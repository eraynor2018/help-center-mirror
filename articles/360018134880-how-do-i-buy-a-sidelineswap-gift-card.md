# How do I buy a SidelineSwap gift card?

Click [here](https://sidelineswap.com/gift-cards/) to purchase a SidelineSwap gift card.

Merely select a gift card amount and then submit a payment. The gift card will be delivered to you via email at which point it can be shared with anyone. Please note that the credit can only be used by the account that claims the gift card.
