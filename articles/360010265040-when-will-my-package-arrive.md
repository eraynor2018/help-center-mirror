# When will my package arrive? 

You will be provided a tracking number and a live tracking link on your swap page as soon as you've made your purchase.

The majority of SidelineSwap Sellers ship their orders within a few days of purchase. However, there are times when the Seller may be on vacation or face other circumstances which prevents them from shipping promptly. We recommend communicating with the Seller for more information regarding shipping time.

Domestic USPS Priority Mail shipments generally take 1-3 business days to deliver. UPS Ground and FedEx Ground generally take 2-6 business days.
