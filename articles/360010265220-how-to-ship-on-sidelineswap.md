# How to Ship on SidelineSwap

![1200687882300377-SidelineSwap-992x500-SDB-1.02.png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/4404755158290/1200687882300377-SidelineSwap-992x500-SDB-1.02.png)

All Sellers are provided prepaid shipping labels upon making a sale. US sellers may be generated a USPS, FedEx, or UPS label and Canadian sellers will receive a Canada Post label. The cost of postage is entirely covered by the Buyer. **Upon making a sale, click "Print Label" from your swap page, print the label, attach the label to your package, and ship!**

If you prefer to ship with your own tracking number, if you're unable to print the provided shipping label, or if your existing label is insufficient, you may purchase a new tracking number and add the tracking number to your swap page. Learn more [here](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/articles/4411935113362). 

The funds in your SidelineSwap Seller Dashboard move from "Pending" to "Available" as soon as the Buyer has approved the sale or automatically 72 hours after the item has arrived at the Buyer's address-- whichever comes first. 

*Don’t have a ride to the post office? [You can schedule a pickup with USPS here](https://tools.usps.com/schedule-pickup-steps.htm) for no charge and [you can schedule a pickup with CanadaPost here](https://www.canadapost.ca/web/en/products/details.page?article=parcel_pickup_for_bu) for a small charge.*

*Need packaging guidance? Learn more [here](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/sections/360003197619-Packaging-Tips-Tricks).*
