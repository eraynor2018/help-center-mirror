# When will my order ship?

Once you make a payment, there will be a live tracking link available on your swap page. 

The majority of SidelineSwap Sellers ship their orders within a couple days of a purchase. However, there are times when the Seller may be on vacation or face other circumstances which prevents them from shipping promptly. We recommend communicating with the Seller for more information regarding shipping time. 

*When you make a purchase, your payment is escrowed, meaning the Seller does not get paid until you've confirmed that the order has arrived in its promised condition or 72 hours after the package has arrived at your address-- whichever comes first. If the Seller never ships the item, then you will be refunded. If the Seller ships the wrong item, you can return the item for a full refund. Your payment is entirely protected.*
