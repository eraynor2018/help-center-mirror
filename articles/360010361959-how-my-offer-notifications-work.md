# How My Offer Notifications Work

You will receive a text and email notification if the offer is accepted, rejected, or if it expires.

If you've downloaded the SidelineSwap App, you will receive a push notification!

You can manage all of your notifications [here](https://sidelineswap.com/settings/notifications).
