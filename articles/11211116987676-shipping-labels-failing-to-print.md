# Shipping Labels Failing to Print

Due to an issue with our shipping label provider, sellers can't currently print their shipping labels. Seller have the option to retry printing the label, so please do so within the next 20-30 minutes. If it doesn't work at that time, please give it another hour or so as our shipping label partner should have the issue resolved soon.
