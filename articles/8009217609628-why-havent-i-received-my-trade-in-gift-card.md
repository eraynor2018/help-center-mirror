# Why haven’t I received my Trade-In gift card? 

You should receive your gift card within 24 hours of trading in your gear at a SidelineSwap Trade-In event. If you have not received your gift card, there are two possible explanations:

* The gift card is in your email’s Spam or Junk folder
* Your email address was misspelled

If after checking your Spam/Junk folders you still cannot find your gift card, **please submit** [**this form**](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=7983208946204). Our Trade-In team will review your email and re-send your gift card within 48 hours.

Looking for more information on SidelineSwap’s Trade-In events? Learn more [here](https://sidelineswap.com/events).
