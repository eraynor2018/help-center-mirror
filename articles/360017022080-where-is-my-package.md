# Where is my package?

Once your payment is complete, you can track your order any time from your **swap page** — you’ll see a **live tracking link** as soon as the seller ships your item.

---

### 🚚 **Typical Shipping Times**

Most sellers ship within **a few days** of purchase, but shipping timelines can vary.  
If your order hasn’t shipped yet, the seller might be traveling or waiting to drop off multiple packages together.

Feel free to **message the seller** from your swap page for an update — they’re usually happy to share a timeline.

---

### 🕵️ **Tracking Updates & Delays**

Sometimes tracking links take time to update. It’s possible your item has been shipped even if it hasn’t received its first scan yet.

If your tracking hasn’t updated:

* Wait a few days — initial scans can be delayed.
* Check that you’re viewing the tracking link on your **swap page** for the most accurate info.
* If it’s been **two weeks** with no movement, reach out to our support team. We can contact the carrier to investigate.

💡 **Note:** SidelineSwap Support sees the same tracking data you do — we can’t view additional information until the carrier flags a shipment as delayed or lost.

---

### ⏳ **When a Package Is Considered Lost**

SidelineSwap can only cancel or refund a shipment in transit once it’s officially considered lost:

| Shipment Type | Considered Lost After |
| --- | --- |
| **Domestic (U.S. & Canada)** | 2 weeks with no tracking updates |
| **International** | 2 months with no tracking updates |

Once that window has passed, we’ll work with our shipping partners to resolve the issue and refund you if necessary.

---

### 💬 **Summary**

* Track your shipment directly from your swap page.
* Contact your seller if your item hasn’t shipped within a few days.
* Tracking updates may lag — wait up to two weeks before reaching out for help.
* We can step in after 3 weeks (domestic) or 2 months (international) with no updates.
