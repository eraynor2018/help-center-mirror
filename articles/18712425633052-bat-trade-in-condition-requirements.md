# Bat Trade-In Condition Requirements

If your bat exhibits any of the following characteristics, it will not be considered for trade-in:

* **Dents & Deformities:** If a dent is larger than a nickel or the barrel has any bending, use a towel to run your hand around the bat to feel for significant dents.
* **Rattling in the Barrel:** Shake the bat near your ear and listen for any rattling sounds inside.
* **Cracks:** Look for large, “shark tooth”-like cracks. Light webbing or minor paint chips are acceptable.
* **End Cap Issues:** Check if the end cap is broken, loose, or twisting by gently inspecting and attempting to rotate it.
* **Grip Condition:** If the grip is missing or severely damaged, it must be replaced before trading in the bat.
* **Counterfeit Bats:** Ensure the bat is authentic and not a counterfeit model
