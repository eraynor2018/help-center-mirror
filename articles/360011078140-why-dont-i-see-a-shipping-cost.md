# Why don't I see a shipping cost?

If you are buying from outside of North America, you may not be provided a shipping quote on specific items, such as hockey sticks and skis. This is because USPS will not ship packages of this size internationally.

We'd recommend looking into a shipping forwarder, including [Shipito](https://www.shipito.com/en/) to process your international purchase.
