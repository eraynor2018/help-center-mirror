# How to Handle a Cashout Failure

## For American Bank Accounts

If you’re experiencing transfer failures, that means you’re entering invalid account and/or routing numbers. You will need valid account and routing numbers to process a successful ACH transfer.

![image.png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/10035895696028)

Please click “Edit Bank Account” on your [Seller Dashboard](https://sidelineswap.com/selling) page and submit valid banking information.

You can find your needed bank information online on your bank's website or on a check book (see visual below!)

![Screenshot 2023-08-16 165731.png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/10035895696284)

## For Canadian Bank Accounts

Please click “Edit Bank Account” on your [Seller Dashboard](https://sidelineswap.com/selling) page and submit valid banking information. You will need your institution, transit, and account number to process a successful ACH transfer.

You can find your needed bank information online on your bank's website or on a check book (see visual below!)
