# SidelineSwap Counterfeit Policy

At SidelineSwap, we take the sale of counterfeit items extremely seriously. The manufacturing and sale of counterfeit goods is illegal and violates intellectual property rights. This policy outlines our approach to handling counterfeit claims and protecting our community.

## **What is a Counterfeit Item?**

A counterfeit item is any product that is made to imitate an authentic item without authorization from the brand owner. This includes unauthorized replicas of branded equipment, apparel, or accessories that are passed off as genuine products.

## **For Buyers**

### **Filing a Dispute on SidelineSwap**

If you believe you've received a counterfeit item:

* Open a dispute within 72 hours of receiving your item on SidelineSwap
* Provide clear evidence supporting your counterfeit claim
* Provide counterfeit verification from the brand manufacturer
* Do not alter, use, or damage the item while the dispute is being investigated

Note: While the 72-hour window is strict, we may make exceptions for counterfeit claims in certain circumstances, provided the item remains unused.

### **Evidence Requirements**

Accepted evidence of counterfeiting may include:

* Authentication from the brand manufacturer
* Detailed photos showing specific signs of counterfeiting
* Comparison photos with authentic items
* Professional authentication documentation

## **For Sellers**

### **First Counterfeit Violation**

We understand that sellers may unknowingly acquire counterfeit items. For first-time violations, we may restore account access if the seller:

* Proves the incident was isolated and unintentional
* Provides valid receipts for their inventory
* Removes all potentially counterfeit items from their listing
* Demonstrates understanding of how to verify authentic items

## **For Sellers**

### **First Counterfeit Violation**

We understand that sellers may unknowingly acquire counterfeit items. For first-time violations, we may restore account access if the seller:

* Proves the incident was isolated and unintentional
* Provides valid receipts for their inventory
* Removes all potentially counterfeit items from their listing
* Demonstrates understanding of how to verify authentic items

### **Second Counterfeit Violation**

A second verified counterfeit violation results in:

* An indefinite account restriction at minimum, often leading to a complete ban/removal from the platform
* Funds from existing sales held for a minimum of 12 months from last delivery

### **Important Notes for Sellers**

* Sellers are not entitled to funds from counterfeit sales
* Release of funds from counterfeit sales is at SidelineSwap's sole discretion
* Sellers are responsible for verifying the authenticity of their items before listing
* Repeated violations may result in legal action

## **Commitment to Authentication**

SidelineSwap is committed to maintaining a trustworthy marketplace for authentic sporting goods. We actively work with brands, sellers, and buyers to prevent the sale of counterfeit items and protect our community's interests.

For questions about this policy or to report suspected counterfeit items, please contact our Trust & Safety team through our Help Center.
