# After Making an Offer

You have the option to make an offer below (or above) a Seller’s asking price by clicking the “Make Offer” on the item page. You will only be charged if the Seller accepts your offer. Once the offer is accepted, the item is yours.

If a Seller does not respond to your offer within 24 hours, the offer expires.
