# Payment Information Security

SidelineSwap uses Braintree to process payments and Payoneer to process bank transfers securely.

You can read more about Braintree [here](https://www.braintreepayments.com/features/data-security) and Payoneer [here](https://www.payoneer.com/enterprise/solutions/fraud-prevention/). Both Braintree and Payoneer are certified to PCI Service Provider Level 1. This is the most stringent level of certification available.
