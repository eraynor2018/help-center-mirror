# What Does Swap "Under Review" Mean?

When a swap is "Under Review,” the Buyer's payment has been successfully processed, but the shipping label is not yet available for the Seller to print. 

The shipping label will become available to the Seller once the SidelineSwap Community team has approved the sale. SidelineSwap will notify both parties once the swap has been approved.
