# How to Confirm a Purchase and Leave Feedback

Once the package is "In Transit" to your address, you will see an "Accept or Reject" link on your swap page.

Once you have received the item you’ve purchased, then you’re ready to finalize your swap. Please click that and follow the process from there. Be sure to leave the Seller feedback!
