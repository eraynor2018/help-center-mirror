# How do I Request a Seel Return?

If you added Seel Return Assurance at checkout, you can start your return any time within **7 days of delivery**.

1. Go to your **swap page**.
2. Click **“Mark as Received”** or **“Accept or Reject Item.”**
3. Select **“No, Request Seel Return.”**
4. You’ll be redirected to Seel’s return portal to complete your request.

Once you submit the request, **Seel handles the rest** — including communication, approval, and your refund. You’ll get updates directly from Seel via email.

💬 Have questions about your Seel return?  
Contact **Seel Support** directly at [support@seel.com](mailto:support@seel.com). They manage all return processing and communication.
