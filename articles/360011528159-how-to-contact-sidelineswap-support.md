# How to Contact SidelineSwap Support

[**Click here to contact SidelineSwap Support**](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/requests/new)

We know the decision to omit phone support is unusual, but after experimenting with different approaches, we’ve found that defaulting to email support enables us to provide the fastest response. We focus on providing high-quality responses, so you’re always talking to someone from our Support team who has an intimate understanding of the platform.  

For the fastest replies, please click the "Contact SidelineSwap Support" link above.
