# Who pays for shipping?

Unless the seller chooses free or discounted shipping when they list their item, the buyer pay sfor shipping during checkout.

Regardless of who pays, after a sale, the Seller receives a prepaid shipping label so all the Seller needs to do is print, pack, and ship.
