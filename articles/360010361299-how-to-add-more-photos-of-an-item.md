# How to Add More Photos of an Item

If a buyer reaches out requesting more photos of your item, you simply need to add the requested picture(s) to your existing listing. We've made this easy for you by adding an "Add Photos" button right to your swap page:

![Screenshot_2023-05-08_at_9.19.25_AM.png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/8725209050908)

Performing this action adds the photo(s) directly to your listing and sends a message to your buyer suggesting they take a look at the listing to view the requested photo(s).

Please note, taking communications off the platform to share pictures is a violation of SidelineSwap's Terms of Use. All communications need to stay on SidelineSwap to be guaranteed SidelineSwap Protection.
