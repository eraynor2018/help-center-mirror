# How do I export my locker data?

![Pill -- Pro.png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/15306877799324)

SidelineSwap offers Sideline Pro+ subscribers the ability to export their locker data for easier management and analysis. Follow the steps below to export your data:

#### **How to Export Locker Data**

1. **Visit Your Locker**: Go to your locker on SidelineSwap.com.
2. **Locate the Export Button**: Find and click the “Export” button.
3. **Request the Report**: After clicking the export button, you’ll receive an email with a link to download the file.

#### **What’s Included in the Locker Export?**

The locker export provides a comprehensive overview of your listed items, including the following fields:

* **Name**: The name of the item.
* **Item ID**: The unique identifier for the item.
* **State**: The current status of the item (e.g., available, sold, draft, etc.).
* **Category 1, Category 2, Category 3**: The hierarchical categories the item belongs to.
* **Brand**: The brand of the item.
* **Condition**: The condition of the item (e.g., new, used).
* **Quality**: The quality assessment of the item (e.g., good, excellent, damaged).
* **Demand**: The demand level for the item.
* **Created At**: The date and time when the item was created.
* **Published At**: The date and time when the item was published.
* **Description**: The detailed description of the item.
* **Original List Price**: The initial listing price of the item.
* **List Price**: The current listing price of the item.
* **Suggested Price**: The suggested price for the item.
* **Markdown**: Any markdowns applied to the item.
* **Gross Margin Est**: The estimated gross margin.
* **Cost**: The cost price of the item.
* ***Coming Soon***
  + **Variation Name**: The name of any variations for the item.
  + **Quantity Listed**: The total quantity listed.
  + **Quantity Sold**: The total quantity sold.
  + **Quantity Available**: The quantity currently available.

By exporting your locker data, you can easily track and manage your inventory, making it simpler to analyze your sales and performance.
