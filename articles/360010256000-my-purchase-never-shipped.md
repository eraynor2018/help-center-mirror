# My Purchase Never Shipped

## What you need to know:

* Sellers don’t get paid until you receive the item
* If an item doesn’t ship, you’ll get a full refund
* After **5 days**, you can cancel if the seller hasn’t printed the label
* After **12 days**, you can cancel regardless of seller activity
* After **21 days**, SidelineSwap automatically cancels and refunds the order

---

## For more information...

If a seller doesn’t ship your item, your money is protected.

Sellers are **not paid** until you receive the item in the promised condition. If an item never ships, you’ll receive a **full refund**.

We automatically remind sellers to ship after payment is submitted, including follow-ups at regular intervals. If the item still doesn’t ship, the order will be canceled and refunded.

You do **not** need to contact support to receive a refund for an unshipped order.

### When you can cancel

You can cancel an unshipped order directly from your **swap page**:

* **After 5 days:** You can cancel if the seller has **not printed the shipping label**
* **After 12 days:** You can cancel **regardless of seller activity**, even if they’ve accessed the label
* **After 21 days:** SidelineSwap automatically cancels and refunds the order

### What to do now

* Visit your **swap page** to check the current order status
* Review the available actions (including canceling, if eligible)

Your payment remains fully protected until the item is delivered.
