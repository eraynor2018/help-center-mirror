# How can I add notes to a swap?

Sideline Pro and Pro+ sellers can keep a private note on any swap, for example "2 yellow grips, 3 black" or "Bin C4". You write the note on the swap page, and it prints on your packing slip so you can read it while you pack.

### How to add a note

1. On SidelineSwap.com, open the swap from your **Swaps** inbox.
2. On the right side of the swap page, below **Print Label** and **View Receipt**, click **Notes**.
3. Type your note. It saves as you type. You'll see **Saved** when it's stored.

To edit a note, open **Notes** again and change the text. To delete it, clear all the text.

### Copy a buyer's message into your note

When a buyer asks for something specific in the conversation, you don't need to retype it:

1. Hover over the buyer's message.
2. Click the **Copy to notes** icon.

The message is added to the end of your note on its own line. Anything already in the note stays.

### Where your note shows up

* **Swap page:** the closed Notes row shows the first line of your note.
* **Swaps inbox:** a small notepad icon marks every swap that has a note.
* **Packing slip:** the note prints in a box on the packing slip. If there's no note, the box prints blank so you can write on it by hand.

### Who can see your note?

Buyers can't see your note on SidelineSwap. **But the packing slip goes in the package**, so if you include the slip, the buyer can read the note. Don't write anything on it that you wouldn't want the buyer to see.

### How long can a note be?

A note can be up to six lines, which is the size of the box on the packing slip. When the note is full, you'll see **Full — six lines fit on the slip**. If a buyer message is too long to fit, you'll see **Too long to fit on the slip**, and your note won't change.
