# Why isn't my swap status updating correctly?

If your swap status doesn't seem to match what's actually happening with your order, here's how to resolve the most common issues.

### My swap is stuck in "Payment Processing"

If your swap has been stuck in a "Payment Processing" state, this isn't something you can resolve on your own — it requires our team to investigate.

Please [contact SidelineSwap support](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=360000525400) and include your swap number so we can look into the payment status and get things moving.

### My swap shipped but the status hasn't updated

If you (or your seller) shipped the item but the swap is still showing as not shipped, try refreshing the tracking status first:

1. Follow the steps in [Refresh Tracking Status](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/articles/6715535838364-What-is-Refresh-Tracking-Status)
2. Wait a few minutes for the status to update

If the status still doesn't update after refreshing, [contact SidelineSwap support](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=360000525400) and include your swap number so we can look into it.

### My swap was delivered but the status hasn't updated

If the carrier shows the item as delivered but your swap still shows as in transit, try refreshing the tracking status first:

1. Follow the steps in [Refresh Tracking Status](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/articles/6715535838364-What-is-Refresh-Tracking-Status)
2. Wait a few minutes for the status to update

If the status still doesn't update after refreshing, [contact SidelineSwap support](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=360000525400) and include your swap number so we can look into it.
