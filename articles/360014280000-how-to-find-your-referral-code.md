# How to Find Your Referral Code

Click [here](https://sidelineswap.com/invite) to access your account's customized referral code.

Each friend of yours who signs up using your referral code will be provided $5 in site credit to be used for purchases on SidelineSwap.

Your account will earn a $5 credit after the individual you've referred completes a qualifying purchase of $50 or more.

Credits earned from referrals can only be used on SidelineSwap and cannot be cashed out. Credits will be added to your available balance which can be viewed on your [Seller Dashboard](https://sidelineswap.com/selling "View your referral credits on your seller dashboard").
