# How to Buy from the UK as of 2021

Due to tax laws enacted by the UK, we cannot support shipments to the UK as of 2021.   
  
This does not mean that you cannot buy from SidelineSwap. We strongly recommend looking into a shipping forwarder, such as [Shipito](https://www.shipito.com/en/), to process your shipment. You would merely add a US-based shipping address, as provided by your shipping forwarder who would then forward the shipment to your UK address.
