# How do I remove an item?

If you're accessing the platform from the App, please click the three dots at the top right of the listing page or the "More" option on the actions for the listing, and then select "Remove Item.” You can also remove a listing directly from your locker by swiping the listing to the left. 

If you're accessing SidelineSwap from web, please hover over the listing from your locker page, and select 'Remove.” From mobile web, click on the trashcan icon on the banner above the item photos on the listing page.
