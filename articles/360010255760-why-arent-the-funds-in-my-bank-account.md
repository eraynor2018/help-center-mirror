# Why aren't the funds in my bank account?

**Remember, first-time cashouts generally take 1-2 business days to process.** If your cashout has not processed after 2 full business days, it's likely that you've entered invalid bank information. When your cashout fails, it takes 3-7 days from the day that you cashed out for the funds to process back into your [SidelineSwap Seller Dashboard](https://sidelineswap.com/selling).

Please click “Edit Bank Account” on your [Seller Dashboard](https://sidelineswap.com/selling) page and submit valid banking information. You can find your needed bank information online on your bank's website or on a checkbook.

If your funds still aren't in your Seller Dashboard after the timeframes above, contact Support with the cashout date and amount.

*After your first successful cashout, all subsequent cashouts take less than 1 business day to process.*
