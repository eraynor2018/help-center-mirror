# How to Package My Item

SidelineSwap’s prepaid shipping label covers the entire cost of shipping. You can use any box or packaging you may have lying around the house to ship your item. Make sure you’ve packaged your item as securely as possible.

For U.S. Sellers: USPS will give you free packaging for Priority Mail shipments unless the item is oversized. Please visit USPS.com for the available Priority Mail packaging options. 

*Note for USPS shipments: Do not use Flat Rate USPS shipping boxes when shipping with a Priority Mail label. Do not use packaging from a competing shipping service; i.e. combining a UPS box with a USPS label. Your package will be returned to the sender.*

*Don’t have a ride to the post office? [You can schedule a pickup with USPS here](https://tools.usps.com/schedule-pickup-steps.htm) for no charge and [you can schedule a pickup with CanadaPost here](https://www.canadapost.ca/web/en/products/details.page?article=parcel_pickup_for_bu) for a small charge.*
