# How to Share Shipping Labels

In the event you're unable to ship your item out yourself, you may need to share the shipping label with a friend or family member. In order to do so, please access your swap from the website version of SidelineSwap.

From the swap page, click "Print Label" as you would if you were shipping the item out yourself:

![Untitled design (30).png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/10036265364380)

Clicking "Print Label" will take you to the standard page from which you can print your shipping label and receipt. From there, click where it says "Hide Instructions":

![Untitled design (31).png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/10036252515996)

The next page you're taken to will be a full-sized version of your shipping label. The URL to that page is a shareable one you can provide to whoever needs to print out your label! Just copy and paste the URL to share your shipping label.
