# How bundle cancellations work

In the event that the buyer and seller have agreed to cancel an existing order to facilitate a bundled order, the seller may cancel the swap from their swap page by selecting "We are bundling an order" after clicking "Cancel Swap".

Please note the refund can take 7-10 days to process back to your bank account. If an order is incorrectly marked as a "Bundle" cancellation, please contact SidelineSwap Support. We can issue you a full refund to your payment method.
