# What No Shipping Costs on an Item Means

In rare cases, some Sellers have disabled shipments to specific locations around the world. If you are not shown a shipping quote despite linking an address to your account, this is likely why.

If you are an international Buyer looking to buy from an American Seller, please consider using [this inexpensive and reliable shipping forwarder service.](https://www.shipito.com/en/)
