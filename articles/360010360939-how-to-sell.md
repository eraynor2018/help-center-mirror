# How to Sell

Listing an item on SidelineSwap couldn't be simpler. [Click here to upload the items you’d like to sell](https://sidelineswap.com/items/new).

Every listing has a “Buy Now” price. A Buyer can purchase your item at the chosen price you assign to the listing. Buyers also have the option to submit an offer below (or above) your asking price. You will have the choice to accept or reject that offer.

Once a Buyer has paid for your item, we’ll send you a prepaid shipping label along with easy-to-follow instructions. The Buyer will be provided with the tracking number affiliated with that shipping label.

The payment will be deposited in your [SidelineSwap Seller Dashboard](https://sidelineswap.com/selling) and you’ll be able to withdraw funds to a bank account once the order has been approved by the Buyer or 72 hours after the package has been delivered. As long as the Buyer doesn't open a dispute, you’ll be able to cash out no more than three days after the delivery date.

[Click here](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010360959-SidelineSwap-Seller-Protection) to find out about how you're protected as a Seller.
