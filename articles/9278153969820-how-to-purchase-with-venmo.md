# How to Purchase with Venmo

Shoppers on SidelineSwap can now purchase using Venmo. Currently, this option only exists on the iOS app, but it is coming soon to SidelineSwap.com!

In order to use Venmo, you must **first have the Venmo app installed**. Once you do, simply click “Add Payment Method” from your [Payment Methods](https://sidelineswap.com/settings/payment-methods) page on the SidelineSwap app or at checkout the next time you purchase an item or submit an offer.

![IMG_1153.png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/9278134725404)               ![IMG_1154.png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/9278119036700)

*Please note that sending payments outside of SidelineSwap checkout (whether by PayPal or any other means) is still a violation of SidelineSwap policy — we want to make sure your purchases are fully protected with [SidelineSwap’s Buyer Protection guarantee.](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010255960-SidelineSwap-Buyer-Protection)*
