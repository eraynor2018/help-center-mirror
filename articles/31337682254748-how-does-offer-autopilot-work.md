# How does Offer Autopilot work?

Offer Autopilot answers offers on your listings for you. You choose a rule once, and Autopilot accepts or counters each new offer based on that rule, so you don't have to respond to every offer yourself.

## Choose how offers are answered

You can pick one of three options:

* **Set a floor price.** Choose how far below your asking price you're willing to go, as a percentage. Offers at or above your floor are accepted. Offers below your floor get a counteroffer at your floor.
* **Firm price.** Offers at your asking price are accepted. Any lower offer gets a counteroffer at your asking price.
* **Let me decide.** Autopilot doesn't answer offers. You respond to each offer yourself, the same as before.

**Example:** Your item is listed at $100 and your floor is 20% off. An offer of $85 is accepted. An offer of $60 gets a counteroffer at $80.

## Turn on Offer Autopilot

1. Go to [**Settings > Offer Autopilot**](https://sidelineswap.com/settings/offer-autopilot).
2. Choose **Set a floor price**, **Firm price**, or **Let me decide**.
3. If you chose a floor price, enter the percentage off your asking price.
4. Save your changes.

Your rule applies to all of your listings, including offers you've already received but haven't answered yet.

## Good to know

* **Your floor follows your price.** The floor is a percentage of your current asking price. If you drop your price, your floor drops with it.
* **Floors are rounded up to the nearest dollar.**
* **There's a minimum floor.** Your floor can be at most 55% off your asking price, and never lower than $3.
* **Low offers get countered, not blocked.** With Autopilot on, a buyer's very low offer gets a counteroffer at your floor instead of being turned away.
* **Auctions aren't included.** Autopilot doesn't respond to auction bids.
* **Offers sold elsewhere are skipped.** If an item sells or is no longer available before Autopilot responds, it doesn't accept the offer.
