# Using "Favorites" for Saved Searches, Lockers, and Items

"Favorites" will allow you to browse through the items that matter to you most. You’ll find sections for:

* Items you've favorited
* Searches you’ve added
* Lockers you follow

How to add to your Favorites:

Go to the listing page and locate the “Favorites” button among the item actions. You can find this next to the "Buy Now" button. 

When the heart appears green, this item has been added to your “My Feed” page.

![Screenshot 2023-08-16 223221.png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/10036397389596)

How to add a specific search to "Favorites":

Select your Sport and Category. Use the filters on the left side of your screen to narrow down your search. Select "Save this search” and set your notification preferences. You can create a nickname for your search to help you find it quickly on the “Favorites” page.

![Screenshot 2023-08-16 222250.png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/10036397389980)

How to “Follow” lockers:

Click the “Follow” button on the top right side of a locker to add them to “Favorites.” You can set your notification preferences. Now, you can view all new listings added to this locker from your “My Feed” page.
