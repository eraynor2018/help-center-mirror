# How to Cancel a Purchase

If you are a Seller trying to cancel a recent sale, click [here](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010361759).

**Trying to cancel an order you placed? Here’s how it works:**

#### 🕐 Before the seller ships

If your item hasn’t been shipped yet, there are a few possibilities:

* **If the Seller hasn’t printed a label after 5 days:**  
  You’ll see the option to **Cancel Swap** right on your swap page.
* **If it’s been 12 days and the item still hasn’t shipped:**  
  You can cancel the order yourself, even if the seller printed a label but didn’t ship it.

#### 💬 How to cancel

* **On web:** Go to your swap page and click **Cancel Swap**.
* **On the app:** Click **More**, then tap **Cancel Swap**.

#### ⏳ Automatic cancellations

SidelineSwap automatically cancels and refunds all unshipped orders after **21 days**, unless both you and the seller have agreed to an extension.
