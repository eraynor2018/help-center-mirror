# Safely Shipping and Receiving Packages

You can continue to ship and receive packages, according to today’s guidance from the [World Health Organization](https://www.who.int/news-room/q-a-detail/q-a-coronaviruses), the [Centers for Disease Control & Prevention](https://www.cdc.gov/coronavirus/2019-ncov/faq.html) the U.S. Surgeon General, and the Public Health Agency of Canada. We will continue to monitor this situation and keep you updated.
