# Why don't I see all my items in my locker?

We have an ongoing issue on SidelineSwap where sellers are unable to see all the items they have for sale in their locker. These items are still available to purchase and buyer scan find them when shopping the main browse experience. We are isolating the issue and hope to have a resolution soon.
