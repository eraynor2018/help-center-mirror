# How do I get reimbursed for offline shipping?

If you have shipped using a different tracking number than the one that was provided to you, SidelineSwap will automatically reimburse you up to the amount paid for by the buyer or you, minus non-refundable processing fees. Currently, you will be paid 88% of the original shipping cost.

You can see proof of this reimbursement in the receipt from your swap page. These funds will be made available to cash out when the swap is completed.

The new tracking number will be added to the swap page for the seller and the buyer to track.
