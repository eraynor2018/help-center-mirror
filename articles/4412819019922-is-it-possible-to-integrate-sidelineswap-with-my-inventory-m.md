# Is it possible to integrate SidelineSwap with my Inventory Management System?

If you sell gear across multiple platforms, we know how important it is to keep your inventory in sync.  
While SidelineSwap doesn’t yet offer a live inventory management system or direct integrations, we’re actively working toward building inventory tools and future integrations that make multi-channel selling easier. As part of that process, it’s incredibly helpful for us to understand which inventory management software our sellers currently use.

If you’re interested in future inventory integrations, or already use an inventory management system you’d like to see supported, we’d love to hear from you. Please reach out to [partnerships@sidelineswap.com](mailto:partnerships@sidelineswap.com) and let us know what tools you’re using.

Your feedback helps shape what we build next.
