# What are the Accepted Payment Methods?

You can purchase items on SidelineSwap using any valid credit card or debit card. You can also use the "Available to Spend" funds in your SidelineSwap Seller Dashboard to purchase on SidelineSwap. 

We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover, JCB, and Diner’s Club. Gift and prepaid cards from these companies are also available for purchase. If you’re using a gift card, you’ll need to type in your name as it appears on the card (ex. “Valued Customer”). Shoppers can also purchase through PayPal, Venmo, Apple Pay, and Sezzle (Buy Now Pay Later). 

Don’t have a card? You can ask a family member’s permission to use theirs. Please remember it’s against the law to use a card without the owner’s permission.
