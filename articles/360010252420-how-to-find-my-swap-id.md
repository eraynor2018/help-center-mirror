# How to Find My Swap ID

If you're accessing the swap from the App, please click the three dots at the top right of your swap page. Otherwise, the Swap ID can be found at the top of the conversation thread in your inbox under the usernames on web or mobile web. ![Untitled design (15).png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/10034878218268)
