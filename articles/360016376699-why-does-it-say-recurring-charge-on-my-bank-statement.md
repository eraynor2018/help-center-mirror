# Why does it say "Recurring Charge" on my bank statement? 

**Every purchase made on SidelineSwap is a one-time payment!**

SidelineSwap does not charge recurring fees. The term “recurring,” as seen in your statement, is a card processing code and does not mean that you are charged on a recurring basis. This code is used to save you time by preventing our users from having to re-enter their credit or debit card information again.
