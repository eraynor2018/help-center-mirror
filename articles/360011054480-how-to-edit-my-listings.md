# How to Edit My Listings

To edit your listing on SidelineSwap:

From desktop web: Click the three dots on the item thumbnail and then "Edit" from the menu. You can also click to "Edit" the listing from the item page itself.

![Item_Page_edit_item.png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/360015220039) 

From mobile web: Click the pencil icon below the listing from the locker view or the pencil above the listing from the item view

From the App: Click "Edit" on the item in your locker or, from the item page itself, click "More" from the action menu and then "Edit Item".
