# What does Fast Shipping mean?

We've introduced the "Fast Shipping" filter to your SidelineSwap search experience. By using the "Fast Shipping" filter, you'll see only results from our sellers who are both classified as fast shippers by SidelineSwap and who are shipping from a location within a few hundred miles or less of your zip code.

While shipments from anywhere across the United States are still making it to their end destination, some mail carriers are experiencing longer than usual delays. This filter will guarantee a quicker delivery time. These shipments are likely to make it to you within 1-4 business days.

For our Canadian buyers, the "Fast Shipping" filter will include only sellers who are located in Canada, as international shipping times typically exceed domestic shipping times.
