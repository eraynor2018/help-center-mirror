# How to close a dispute

If you and your seller have come to a resolution, you may close the dispute from your swap page. Your seller may issue a partial refund from their swap page prior to the closing of your dispute.

To close a dispute and release the funds to the seller, click "Close Dispute" from your swap page and confirm that you wish to close the dispute. From the iOS App, click "View Dispute" and close the dispute or click the three dots at the top right of your swap page and close the dispute.

Please note that once a dispute is closed, the swap is completed, and the dispute cannot be re-opened.
