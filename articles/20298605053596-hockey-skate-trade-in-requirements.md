# Hockey Skate Trade-In Requirements

* Blade holder, boot, and steel cannot have any cracks
* Skates must have 1/2” of blade
* Skates must have insoles
* Skates cannot have any mold or mildew
* Skates must have laces in good condition
* Achilles guard should not be able to be bent >45 degrees back
* Skates must have all rivets attaching the blade holder to boot
* Eyelets must not be ripped or broken
* Steel cannot have excessive rust
