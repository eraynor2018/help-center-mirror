# How do I auto-reject lowball offers?

![Pill -- Pro.png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/15306850374812)

Sideline Pro+ subscribers can set an auto-reject threshold on their listings, which automatically rejects lowball offers. This feature saves time and allows sellers to focus on serious buyers.

#### **How do I set an auto-reject threshold?**

You can configure the auto-rejection feature during the listing process or while editing an existing item. Here's how:

1. **Navigate to the List Flow**:
   * Whether you're listing a new item or editing an existing one, go to the listing page. .
2. **Set the Auto-Reject Percentage**:
   * Find the option to set a percentage for auto-rejecting offers.
   * Enter the percentage below your asking price that you consider a lowball offer. For example, setting it to 10% means offers more than 10% below your asking price will be automatically rejected.

#### **How Auto-Rejection Works**

* Buyers can still *submit* offers lower than the percentage you've set, but you will not be notified when these come through.
* If an offer is below your auto-reject threshold, it will be automatically rejected after 3 minutes, making it seem like it was touched and evaluated by a human.
* You will receive a notification once an offer has been auto-rejected in case you’d like to continue the negotiation.

By using the auto-rejection feature for lowball offers, you can enhance your selling efficiency and focus on valuable negotiations.
