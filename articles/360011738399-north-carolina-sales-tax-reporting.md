# North Carolina Sales Tax Reporting

As a resident of North Carolina, you're entitled to request a monthly sales report from SidelineSwap, indicating gross sales and your total number of sales.

If you require such a report, please click this [link](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/requests/new), select "Seller Dashboard" under "Category," and in the description of the request indicate that you require a "North Carolina Sales Report."
