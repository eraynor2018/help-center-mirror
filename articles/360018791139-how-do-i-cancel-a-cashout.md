# How do I cancel a cashout?

You may cancel your first cashout within **three hours** of submitting the cashout directly from your [Seller Dashboard](https://sidelineswap.com/selling).

After three hours, the cashout cannot be canceled by your account or by SidelineSwap. If you entered invalid bank information, the cashout will fail within 3-5 business days, at which point you can cash out to your correct bank account.

Please click “Edit Bank Account” on your [Seller Dashboard](https://sidelineswap.com/selling) page and submit valid banking information. You can find your needed bank information online on your bank's website or on a checkbook. (see visual below!)
