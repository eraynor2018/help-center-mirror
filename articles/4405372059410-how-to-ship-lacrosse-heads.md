# How to Ship Lacrosse Heads

[SidelineSwap](https://sidelineswap.com/shop/skiing/skis) is a great place to buy and sell new or used lacrosse heads. Below are some tips to help you ship your lacrosse head safely and efficiently.

Materials You'll Need for a Head

* A small or medium sized mailing box, available at [FedEx](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/packing/supplies/boxes.html#standard) or available for free with a [USPS Priority Mail label.](https://store.usps.com/store/product/shipping-supplies/priority-mail-box-4-P_O_BOX4)
* Bubble wrap, available at [FedEx](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/packing/supplies/near-me.html#cushioning-void-fill-packing-peanuts) or available at any office supply store or general merchandise store (i.e. Target, Walmart)
* Quality packing tape, available at [FedEx](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/packing/supplies/near-me.html#packing-tape) or available at any office supply store or general merchandise store (i.e. Target, Walmart)
* Cardboard scraps, available from previously used packaging of any sort
* Newspaper, old magazines, parchment paper, available at any office supply store or general merchandise store (i.e. Target, Walmart)

Package Your Head

1. Take your bubble wrap and wrap it around the head and tape it in place.
2. Place cardboard scraps, crumpled magazine sheets, newspaper, and or parchment paper in the bottom of the box and place the head inside.
3. Add more cardboard scraps, crumpled magazine sheets, newspaper, and or parchment paper on top of the head to fill the box and keep the head from moving around.
4. Tape up the box, place you shipping labels on it, and ship it to its new owner!
