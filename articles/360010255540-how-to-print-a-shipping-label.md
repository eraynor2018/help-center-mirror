# How to Print a Shipping Label

Once you’ve made a sale, you can print your prepaid shipping label from the offer details page. Click “Print Shipping Label” and then print the label.

You can adjust the size of the label in “Print Preview.” Make sure the entire barcode and both addresses are visible on the printed page.
