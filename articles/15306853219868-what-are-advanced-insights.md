# What are “Advanced Insights”?

![Pill -- Pro (1).png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/15306816940956)![Pill -- Pro.png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/15306816941212)

The "Insights" feature on SidelineSwap provides valuable data to help you make informed decisions about pricing and selling your items. With Insights, you can see the average sales price, sales volume, and sell-through rate for similar items across the marketplace as well as a detailed history of offers and price adjustments for your item.

#### **Where to Find the Insights Feature**

You can access the Insights feature in three main areas on SidelineSwap:

1. **Within Your Locker**: Navigate to your locker and click on the “Insights” button next to any item you wish to analyze.
2. **On Item Pages**: When viewing a specific item’s page, find and click the “Insights” button to get detailed information about that item.
3. **On Swap Pages**: While viewing a swap page, the “Insights” button will provide you with data relevant to the items involved in the swap.

#### **What Information Does Insights Provide?**

The Insights feature offers comprehensive data to help you optimize your selling strategy:

* **Average Sales Price**: See the average price at which similar items have sold on SidelineSwap. This helps you set a competitive and realistic price for your item.
* **Sales Volume**: Get an overview of the sales volume for similar items to understand  how frequently these items sell.
* **Sell-Through Rate**: Understand the sell-through rate, which shows the percentage of similar items that have been sold compared to the total listed. This metric helps gauge the demand for your item.
* **Offer and Price History**: View a detailed history of offers received and any price adjustments for your item. This helps you track how your item’s price and interest have changed over time.

#### **How to Use Insights for Better Selling**

By leveraging the data provided by  the Insights feature, you can make informed pricing decisions. s. This can lead to faster sales and better profitability.

* **Set Competitive Prices**: Use the average sales price to set your item’s price at a level that is attractive to buyers while ensuring you achieve a fair return.
* **Monitor Demand**: Sales volume and sell-through rate data help you understand the demand for your item, allowing you to adjust your strategy accordingly.
* **Track Offers**: Keep an eye on the offers and price adjustments history to identify trends and make data-driven decisions on future price changes.
