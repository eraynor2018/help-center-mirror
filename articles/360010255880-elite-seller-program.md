# "Elite Seller" Program

The "Elite Seller" badge is a distinction reserved for only our best Sellers. An "Elite Seller" must not only sell a ton of gear, but respond to **all** messages and ship quickly too.

To be eligible, you must have 50+ completed sales with fewer than 3 dropped sales (unless the refund was issued at the Buyer's request) or 25+ sales with no dropped sales.

Sellers with a proven track record on other platforms may also qualify.

SidelineSwap reserves the right to assign and remove the "Elite Seller" badge from any account at any time.
