# How to Use PayPal

**You may use PayPal to make purchases on SidelineSwap.**

To do so, merely click “Add Payment Method” from your [Payment Methods](https://sidelineswap.com/settings/payment-methods) page or from the “Review Your Order” page the next time you purchase an item or submit an offer.

Please note that sending payments outside of SidelineSwap checkout (whether by PayPal or any other means) is still a violation of SidelineSwap policy — we want to make sure your purchases are fully protected with [SidelineSwap’s Buyer Protection guarantee.](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010255960-SidelineSwap-Buyer-Protection) 

While you may now make purchases using PayPal on SidelineSwap, we do not offer the option to cash out your earnings to PayPal. This is a buying only feature.
