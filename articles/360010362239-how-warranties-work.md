# How Warranties Work

SidelineSwap does not manufacture, store, or inspect any of the items sold through our Services.

SidelineSwap provides the venue; the items in our marketplaces are produced, listed, and sold directly by independent Sellers, so SidelineSwap cannot and does not issue warranties for items that arrive as advertised and subsequently break or suffer damage.  

We encourage our Buyers to follow up with the Seller (the previous owner of the item) regarding warranties. 

As our [Buyer Protection Policy](https://help.sidelineswap.com/buying/buyer-faqs/buyer-protection) explains, you are eligible for a refund if the item you receive *arrives* in a different condition than advertised.
