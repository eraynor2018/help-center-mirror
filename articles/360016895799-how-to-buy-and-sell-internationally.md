# How to Buy and Sell Internationally

SidelineSwap supports sellers from the United States and Canada. If you are located outside of these two countries, you cannot sell from SidelineSwap at this time.

Buyers can purchase from most of the world. [Link your address to your account](https://sidelineswap.com/settings/addresses) and you'll get a shipping quote directly on each item page, without having to link your payment info.

One exception: as of September 8, 2026, Canada's tariffs on US goods mean many items from US sellers can't ship to Canadian addresses. [More on that here](#).
