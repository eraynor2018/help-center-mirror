# How to Buy and Sell Internationally

SidelineSwap supports sellers from the United States and Canada. If you are located outside of these two countries, you cannot sell from SidelineSwap at this time.

You can purchase from anywhere in the world! [Link your address to your account](https://sidelineswap.com/settings/addresses) and you'll be provided a shipping quote directly on each item page, without having to link your payment info.
