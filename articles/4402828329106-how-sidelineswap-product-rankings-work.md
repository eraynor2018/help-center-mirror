# How SidelineSwap Product Rankings Work

Our product recommendation system is designed to help you discover the best gear available—fast. It’s powered by a combination of recent sales trends, inventory availability, and sell-through performance, all tuned to reflect what real buyers are loving right now.

Behind the scenes, our team continuously evaluates thousands of items to ensure our rankings highlight popular, high-performing products with strong availability. We look at how well items are selling, how many are currently in stock, and how efficiently products are moving through our marketplace.

We also apply thoughtful data modeling techniques to standardize the information across categories—so you’re always seeing the most relevant and up-to-date recommendations, whether you’re shopping for baseball bats or hockey skates.

While the system is largely data-driven, human expertise plays a key role in shaping and refining how products are evaluated and ranked. Our goal is to make it easier for you to find trusted gear that’s performing well with other buyers—so you can shop with confidence.
