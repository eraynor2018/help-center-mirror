# How to Send Private Offers

Sellers can now make the first move and submit offers to their buyers directly from their swap inbox.

If a buyer has reached out about one of your items, simply click "Send Private Offers" from your swap page view. On iOS, click the heart button labeled "Send Offers". Once you've entered the offer amount, click "Send Offer". The offer amount does not include shipping or tax costs that will be covered by the buyer.

Additionally, sellers can send offers to any user who has favorited their item, before that user even reaches out. As soon as a user favorites your item, a swap will be created in your "Recent Favorites" folder.

*Please note that seller offers aren't available for items that are on sale.*
