# Shipping Regions on SidelineSwap

Shipping Costs

Shipping cost varies by the weight of the package and distance between Buyer and Seller. We offer Buyers commercial shipping rates at checkout, and default to the cheapest shipping option.

If you come across a shipping quote that you think may be excessive, please contact our Support Team. We'll be happy to take a look!
