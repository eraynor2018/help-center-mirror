# Where can I find a receipt for my purchase?

We email you a receipt of your SidelineSwap purchase within minutes of submitting your payment. If you cannot locate the email, please search for [updates@play.sidelineswap.com](mailto:updates@play.sidelineswap.com) in your inbox.

We also provide you a receipt directly from your swap page. Merely click "View Receipt" from your swap page to access the receipt.
