# How to File a Claim for an Insured Item

***For information on how to insure your items, read here.***

If you added insurance to a shipment that is now lost or damaged, you can file a claim through [Seel Worry-Free Delivery](https://www.seel.com/faq/learn-more-about-seel-worry-free-delivery) to recoup your earnings from the sale. To submit your insurance claim, simply enter your email address & swap ID [here](http://resolve.seel.com/sidelineswap) to locate your shipment:

![Screenshot 2026-04-29 at 11.04.35 AM.png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/27128748355356)

After finding your shipment, Click "Report a shipping issue" and follow the next steps with your relevant information to submit your claim.

![Screenshot 2026-04-29 at 11.51.43 AM.png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/27129530353692)

Once your claim has been approved, you will be paid out to any bank account or major e-wallet. Please note, claims typically take about 5 days to be approved.

**Allowed Windows for Filing a Claim**

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Coverage Type** | **Window Open** | **Window Closed** |
| Loss | 30 days after the order was shipped | 90 days after the sale was made |
| Damage | The day the order was delivered | 7 days after the order was delivered |
| Theft | The day the order was delivered | 7 days after the order was delivered |
