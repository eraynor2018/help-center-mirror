# How do I send offers automatically when someone favorites my item?

When someone favorites one of your listings, Offer Autopilot can send them a private offer at your floor price right away. It's a quick way to reach buyers who are already interested.

## Turn it on

1. Go to [**Settings > Offer Autopilot**](https://sidelineswap.com/settings/offer-autopilot).
2. Choose **Set a floor price** and enter your percentage.
3. Turn on **Send offers to favorites**.
4. Save your changes.

This setting turns on automatically when you choose a floor price. You can turn it off at any time.

## How it works

* The offer is sent at that listing's floor price. For example, a $100 item with a 20% floor sends an offer at $80.
* Each buyer gets one offer per listing. If they favorite the same item again, they don't get another offer.
* It only works with **Set a floor price**. With **Firm price** or **Let me decide**, no offer is sent.
* It doesn't send offers on listings you've set to **Let me decide**.
