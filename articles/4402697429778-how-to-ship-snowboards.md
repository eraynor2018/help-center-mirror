# How to Ship Snowboards

[SidelineSwap](https://sidelineswap.com/shop/skiing/skis) is a great place to buy and sell new or used snowboards. We know that packaging snowboards can be a challenge so we wanted to offer you a few tips to help you ship yours safely and efficiently. We've provided instructions on how to ship a snowboard without and without bindings.

**WITH BINDINGS**

Materials You'll Need

* Snowboard box
* Bubble wrap, available at [FedEx](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/packing/supplies/near-me.html#cushioning-void-fill-packing-peanuts) or available at any office supply store or general merchandise store (i.e. Target, Walmart)
* Quality packing tape, available at [FedEx](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/packing/supplies/near-me.html#packing-tape) or available at any office supply store or general merchandise store (i.e. Target, Walmart)
* Scissors



Package Your Snowboard

1. If you're using a snowboard box, roll the snowboard in the bubble wrap or old sheet/towel like a sleeping bag and tape around both ends of the snowboard to ensure that the padding stays in place. Wrap around the snowboard 1-2x.

![6F5FF567-B291-4280-9D33-322F1123A721_1_105_c.jpeg](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/4404440038930)

If you're using bubble wrap instead of a box, wrap tape one end of the bubble wrap to the inside of the toe strap of the binding. Then wrap the entires binding around multiple times and tape in place.

![75720DEE-5F89-4A73-BCB8-97EF7444546B_1_105_c.jpeg](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/4404434569106)![3F4820A7-1555-4D27-9CCE-F4CD1D2CA3E5_1_105_c.jpeg](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/4404440010258)

2. Place snowboard in the box and tape the box.

If you're using bubble wrap instead of a box, wrap around the board a few times, starting at the top of the board.

![FA45C3D1-89BD-4A4A-91F8-90C8F4A68E03_1_105_c.jpeg](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/4404440033042)![CA3104D2-81D0-4CE3-BB8D-731EBD978002_1_105_c.jpeg](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/4404439984402)

3. Attach the shipping label to the package and send the snowboard to its new owner!

**WITHOUT BINDINGS**

Materials You'll Need

* Snowboard box or a DIY "Burrito Box" (made from a medium-sized moving box)
* Bubble wrap, available at [FedEx](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/packing/supplies/near-me.html#cushioning-void-fill-packing-peanuts) or available at any office supply store or general merchandise store (i.e. Target, Walmart)
* Quality packing tape, available at [FedEx](https://www.fedex.com/en-us/shipping/packing/supplies/near-me.html#packing-tape) or available at any office supply store or general merchandise store (i.e. Target, Walmart)
* Scissors



Package Your Snowboard

1. Take your medium-sized moving box and cut open one side flap so that it lays open.

![Screen_Shot_2021-07-02_at_1.06.10_AM.png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/4403189016594)![blobid0.png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/4403182655378)

2. Wrap the snowboard with a layer of bubble wrap and place it in the middle of the open box.

![BCF9FD9B-7F53-4F3C-A0AF-9E60A4744E9D_1_105_c.jpeg](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/4404440059282)

3. Fold the two middle flaps inwards and tape in place. Then fold the top and bottom flaps in before folding the sides of the flaps in (like a burrito).

![063339F3-86CA-41D1-863B-FA9486A00C9E_1_105_c.jpeg](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/4404434686482)![CC9A7085-E56B-4157-BF3E-B83E323EB8BE_1_105_c.jpeg](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/4404440083730)

4. Fold in the sides of the top and bottom flaps and tape them down. Use a lot of tape around to ensure that the packaging does not come undone in-transit.

![6231B350-F2EF-4585-825C-314936B704AF_1_105_c.jpeg](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/4404434713618)

5. Attach the shipping label to the package and send the snowboard to its new owner!

**FedEx/UPS packing service**

Don't have the time for all of this? Not a problem. You can stop by your local FedEx or UPS Authorized ShipCenter where, for a small fee, a packing expert can package your item for you. If you were provided a USPS label, you can still ship with FedEx or UPS. Simply update your new tracking number from your swap page once you have it!
