# Why am I receiving a pin to log in?

For security purposes, if we do not recognize the device that you're attempting to log in from, we will send a code/pin to the email address associated with your account. You need to use this code to access your account. You should not have to do this more than one time per device.

If you did not submit this login request, please reset your password as instructed in the email. The individual who attempted to gain access to your account is unable to do so without the unique security code sent to your email. Your account is secure.

If you cannot find the email in your inbox, please search your inbox for the email from [updates@play.sidelineswap.com](mailto:updates@play.sidelineswap.com). Please check your spam folders as well.
