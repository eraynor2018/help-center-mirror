# How to Disclose Product Deficiencies as a Seller

We put the onus on the Seller to share every deficiency related to the product that they're selling. We do not consider it to be the Buyer's obligation to inquire. 

For Example: If a pair of hockey or lacrosse gloves have small holes in the palm, it is the Seller's obligation to include that detail in their item description. Failure to disclose deficiencies can lead to disputes on your swaps.
