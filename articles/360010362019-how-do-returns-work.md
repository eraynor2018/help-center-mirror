# How do returns work?

## 🔄 **How Do Returns Work**

SidelineSwap offers two types of buyer protection:

1. **SidelineSwap Buyer Protection** — covers items that don’t arrive as advertised.
2. **Seel Return Assurance** — lets you return items that *do* arrive as advertised, for any reason.

Read below to see how each works.

---

### 🚫 **Items That Don’t Arrive as Advertised**

If your item arrives broken, missing pieces, or not as described, you can open a **dispute** for a refund.

1. Within **72 hours of receiving the item**, go to your **swap page.**
2. Click **“Mark as Received”** or **“Accept or Reject Item.”**
3. Select **“No, Not as Advertised”** and explain what’s wrong.

Once a dispute is reviewed and determined to be valid, you’ll have two options:

1. **Negotiate a partial refund**

   * Message the seller from your swap page to agree on an amount.
   * Once both sides confirm, SidelineSwap will process the refund to your card.
   * *Recommended:* This avoids return shipping and gets you a faster resolution.
2. **Return for a full refund**

   * You’ll receive a **prepaid return shipping label** if it’s determined the item wasn’t as advertised.
   * Once the item is returned, you’ll get a full refund.

💡 **Note:** Orders become non-refundable as soon as you approve the sale, or **72 hours after delivery**—whichever comes first. Items that have been **used** also can’t be disputed.

SidelineSwap is built on a community of honest sellers, and most are quick to work with buyers to make things right.

---

### ✅ **Items That Arrive as Advertised**

If your item matches the listing description but isn’t quite what you wanted—say it doesn’t fit, or you changed your mind—the seller isn’t required to accept a return, though they can choose to.

If the seller does agree to take the item back:

* You’ll be refunded the **item cost only**.
* Original shipping and return shipping are **not refundable**.
* For this reason, we generally recommend **relisting the item in your locker** to resell it.

---

### 💡 **Returning Items with Seel Return Assurance**

If you purchased **Seel Return Assurance** at checkout, you’re covered — even if your item arrived exactly as described.

With Seel, you can:

* Return any item for any reason within **7 days of delivery**, as long as it’s still in usable condition.
* Request a return right from your **swap page** by selecting **“No, Request Seel Return.”**
* You’ll then be redirected to Seel’s return portal to complete the process.

Once your return is submitted, **Seel handles everything directly**, including communication and your refund.

🧾 **Note for sellers:** Seel accepts the return, so your payout isn’t affected — buyers get their refund, and you still get paid.

Didn’t add Seel this time? Consider it for your next purchase for extra flexibility and peace of mind.

### ⚖️ **Quick Comparison**

|  | **SidelineSwap Buyer Protection** | **Seel Return Assurance** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Covers** | Items *not as advertised* | Any item, for any reason (if still usable) |
| **Return window** | Within **72 hours** of delivery | Within **7 days** of delivery |
| **Cost** | Free | Small, non-refundable fee added at checkout |
| **Refund issued by** | SidelineSwap | Seel |
| **Seller impact** | Seller covers return if item not as advertised | Seller keeps earnings; Seel covers refund |

---

### 💬 **Summary**

* **Not as described?** Open a dispute within 72 hours for a refund or return.
* **As described, but not right for you?** If you bought Seel, you can return it within 7 days.
* **Didn’t add Seel?** You can still message the seller to see if they’ll accept a return — or simply relist it in your locker.
