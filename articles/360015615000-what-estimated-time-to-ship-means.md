# What Estimated Time to Ship Means

Every account with a selling history has an "Estimated Time to Ship" visible on their locker page. If the account's "Time to Ship" is "within 1 business day", then that seller, on average, ships their package out within 1 business day. **This does not mean the package will arrive within 1 business day.**

The speed of the shipment depends on the selected shipping service and the distance between the buyer and seller.
