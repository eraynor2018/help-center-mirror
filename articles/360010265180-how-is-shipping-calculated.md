# How is shipping calculated?

Shipping cost varies by weight of the package and distance between Buyer and Seller. We offer Buyers commercial shipping rates at checkout, and default to the cheapest shipping option. For most American domestic shipments, we use USPS Priority Mail. For Canadian shipments, we use Canada Post.

SidelineSwap also generates FedEx and UPS shipping labels for domestic shipments within the U.S. when these carriers provide better rates.
