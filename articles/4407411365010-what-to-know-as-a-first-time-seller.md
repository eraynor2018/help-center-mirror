# What to know as a first-time seller

* **Communication is key!**
  + Let your buyer know when you plan to ship their item. Be sure to remain communicative throughout the shipping process.
* **Print your prepaid label**  
  + Click “Print Label” from your swap page. The buyer’s already paid for shipping!
* **Package your item**

  + Need packaging tips? Click [here](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/sections/360003197619-Packaging-Tips-Tricks "https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/sections/360003197619-Packaging-Tips-Tricks") to learn more.
  + Want to insure your package? It’s not too late. Click [here](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010375179-How-to-insure-your-shipments "https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010375179-How-to-insure-your-shipments") to learn more.
* **Ship the package**
  + Schedule a free pickup, leave the package for your local mail carrier, or drop the package at your local carrier. Let your buyer know that the package is shipped.
* **Collect your earnings**
  + The funds in your [Seller Dashboard](https://sidelineswap.com/selling "https://sidelineswap.com/selling") move from "Pending" to "Available" as soon as the buyer has approved the sale or automatically 72 hours after the item has arrived at the buyer's address-- whichever comes first. Once your funds become available, you're clear to [cash out](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010255240-How-to-cash-out).
* **You're protected**
  + Click [here](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010360959) to learn about how you're protected as a SidelineSwap Seller!
