# How to Buy Multiple Items from One Seller

Reach out to the Seller by clicking "Message Seller" on their item page and request that they make one listing for all of the items from their locker that you wish to purchase! That way, you only have to pay for shipping once.

Be sure to remind them that they will need to adjust the dimensions and weight of their listing, accordingly.
