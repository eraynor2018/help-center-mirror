# How do I automatically bump my items?

![Pill -- Pro (1).png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/15306831895708)![Pill -- Pro.png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/15306824485148)

Auto-bumps is  a powerful feature that  keeps your listings visible and fresh ensuring they reach the maximum number of potential buyers.

#### **How Auto-Bumps Work**

* **Automatic Refresh**: Every 28 days after your item is last bumped or published, it will be automatically bumped, bringing it back to the top of the item feed
* **Scheduled Timing**: The auto-bump happens precisely 28 days later at the same time your item was initially published. For example, if you publish an item on a Monday at 7 PM, it will be auto-bumped 4 weeks later on Monday at 7 PM.

#### **Who can use Auto-Bumps?**

Auto-bumps are limited to Sideline Pro subscribers. Review below for the difference in auto-bump usage for each subscription:

* **Sideline Pro Subscribers**:
  + Receive **50 auto-bumps per month**.
  + Items are auto-bumped in the order they were published, ensuring a fair and consistent rotation.
* **Sideline Pro+ Subscribers**:
  + Enjoy **unlimited auto-bumps**, allowing you to keep all your listings prominently displayed without any limitations.

#### **Why Use Auto-Bumps?**

* **Increased Visibility**: Regularly bumped listings stay at the top of the item feed, increasing their visibility to buyers.
* **Convenience**: Auto-bumps remove the need for manual intervention, saving you time and effort while keeping your listings active.

#### **How to Enable Auto-Bumps**

Auto-bumps are automatically enabled for all Sideline Pro and Pro+ subscribers. Simply subscribe to your  desired plan and your listings will be auto-bumped according to the outlined schedule.

#### **Managing Your Auto-Bumps**

* Check your subscription plan to see how many auto-bumps you have remaining (for Sideline Pro subscribers).
* Ensure your listings are up-to-date and well-presented to make the most of the increased visibility from auto-bumps.
