# Banned Accounts

SidelineSwap reserves the right to ban any account for a violation of our Community Guidelines.

Please click [here](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010356299) to learn about our Community Guidelines.

If you believe your account was banned in error, please contact SidelineSwap Support.
