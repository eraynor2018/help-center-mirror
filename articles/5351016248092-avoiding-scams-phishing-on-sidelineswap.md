# Avoiding Scams & Phishing on SidelineSwap

Online scams and phishing attempts are a growing concern. This article provides clear steps to help you identify and avoid phishing scams, such as fraudulent messages claiming to confirm transactions or requiring payment details.

#### **Example of a Phishing Attempt:**

You may receive a message like this:

"We’re pleased to inform you that your item's transaction has been completed, including the price and shipping fees. To move forward, please confirm the details by clicking the link below:<https://sidelineswap.com@s3.id743223.world/TrC4RIrhC>. Your payment details confirmation is necessary due to a recent increase in fraudulent activities."

**This message is fraudulent**. Do not click the link or provide any personal information.

### **How to Identify a Legitimate SidelineSwap Communication:**

1. **Official Communication Channels:** SidelineSwap will only communicate with users via our in-platform messaging system (@**Sidebot**) or the following email addresses:
   * support@sidelineswap.zendesk.com
   * updates@notice.sidelineswap.com
   * updates@play.sidelineswap.com
   * updates@mail1.sidelineswap.com
2. If you receive an email or message claiming to be from SidelineSwap but it is not from Sidebot or the addresses listed above, it is a scam.
3. **Links and URLs:**
   * Legitimate SidelineSwap links always begin with https://sidelineswap.com/. If a link appears suspicious or includes additional domains (e.g., s3.id743223.world), do not click on it.
   * Always check the URL in your browser’s address bar before entering any personal information.
4. **Requests for Personal Information:**
   * SidelineSwap will never ask you to confirm your payment details via an external link.
   * We will not require you to purchase gift cards to authenticate your account, remove selling limits, or enable instant payment.
5. **Offsite Communication:**
   * If a user asks to communicate outside SidelineSwap (e.g., via email or phone), this is a red flag. Keep all communications within SidelineSwap for your protection.

### **Common Scam Indicators:**

* Messages urging immediate action or warning of account suspension unless you click a link.
* Requests for personal or financial information via email or an offsite link.
* Offers that seem too good to be true.

### **What to Do If You Receive a Suspicious Message:**

1. **Do Not Click Links:** Avoid clicking any links in messages or emails that seem suspicious.
2. **Report the Message:** Use the "Report" button on SidelineSwap to flag suspicious users or messages.
3. **Verify with Support:** If you’re unsure, contact SidelineSwap Support via our [Help Center](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=360000525400).

### **Stay Safe and Informed:**

Scammers are constantly evolving their tactics, but by staying vigilant and following the guidelines above, you can help protect yourself and the SidelineSwap community.
