# Avoiding Scams & Phishing on SidelineSwap

Scammers contact SidelineSwap sellers by message and by email, and the story changes every few weeks. Two rules catch all of them.

### **The two rules**

1. **If it's real, it's in your account.** Every real sale, offer, account restriction and credit shows in your SidelineSwap account. If a message or email tells you about one and you can't see it when you log in yourself, it is fake.
2. **We never ask for your details through a link.** SidelineSwap will never ask you to enter card, bank, password or Social Security details through a link, form, QR code, download or message from another user. You never need to verify your account, pay a fee or add money anywhere to receive a sale.

### **How do I check if a sale is real?**

Open SidelineSwap yourself, in the app or by typing sidelineswap.com into your browser, and look at your Seller Dashboard. Do not use a link, button or QR code from the message to get there.

* The sale is there: it is real. Ship it using the prepaid label on its swap page.
* The sale is not there: your item did not sell. Do not ship anything and do not click anything.

An order or invoice number in an email proves nothing. Only a sale in your account counts.

### **How do I know a message or email is really from SidelineSwap?**

We contact you in two ways only.

On SidelineSwap, official messages come from **@Sidebot**. Accounts with names like "sidehelp", "sideline-help" or "sideline\_bot" are not us, even if they use our logo.

By email, we only send from these addresses:

* support@sidelineswap.zendesk.com
* updates@notice.sidelineswap.com
* updates@play.sidelineswap.com
* updates@mail1.sidelineswap.com

An email from any other address that claims to be SidelineSwap is a scam. Real SidelineSwap links always begin with https://sidelineswap.com/. Lookalikes such as sidelineswap.com.au, or links to Google Docs, Google Forms or Google Drive, are not ours.

### **I already clicked the link, scanned the code or entered my information. What do I do?**

Clicking a link or scanning a code does not by itself mean your account was compromised. What matters is what you entered afterward.

1. Close the page. Do not enter anything further or download any files.
2. If you entered your SidelineSwap password or a login PIN, reset your password from the [login page](https://sidelineswap.com/login), then check that the email, phone number and payout details on your account are still yours.
3. If you entered card or bank details, contact your card issuer or bank right away and tell them the details were entered on a fraudulent site.
4. If you downloaded or installed something, remove it and run a security scan on your device.
5. If you entered any login, card, bank or Social Security details, contact support through the [Help Center](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=360000525400) and tell us what you entered. We can lock your account and hold any cashout while we check it.

If you did not enter any information, you do not need to do anything or contact us.

### **Do I need to report a scam message or email?**

* **An email:** no. You do not need to report it or contact us. Ignore it, mark it as spam in your email inbox, and delete it.
* **A message that has disappeared:** no. We have already removed the account that sent it.
* **A message, user or sale on SidelineSwap:** click "Report" on the swap page.
* **A listing:** click "Report" on the item page.

We review these reports regularly, so you do not need to contact support as well. The exception is if you entered personal or payment details; see the question above.

### **Why did a buyer's message disappear from my inbox?**

When we remove an account for suspected fraud or spam, we also remove every message it sent. A message that vanishes, or shows "removed by SidelineSwap", came from an account we have already shut down. Your listing and your account are not affected, and you do not need to do anything.

### **Why am I getting so many "Is this still available?" messages from new accounts?**

Scammers create batches of new accounts and message many sellers at once, often following up with a link. We remove these accounts as we find them. You can ignore the messages. Never click a link sent in reply, and wait for a real offer or sale to appear in your account.

### **Can I block a user?**

It is not currently possible to block another user on SidelineSwap. Report the user instead, and ignore further messages. If someone will not stop contacting you, contact support and we will step in.

### **How did a scammer get my email address?**

SidelineSwap does not share your email address with other users. Scammers usually guess addresses from usernames and other public information, then send the same email to many people at once. Getting one does not mean anyone has accessed your account.

An email from a "buyer" is not how SidelineSwap sales work. Buyers can only contact you through SidelineSwap messages, and every real sale appears in your account. Ignore the email and mark it as spam.

If your username is the same as the first part of your email address, consider changing your username so it is harder to guess.

### **I got a message saying my account is restricted, suspended or under review. Is it real?**

Log in yourself and check. A real restriction shows in your account and comes from @Sidebot or one of the email addresses above. We never ask you to click a link and enter card details to lift a restriction. If your account works normally, the message was fake.

### **A buyer or seller asked me to pay, or talk, outside SidelineSwap. Is that OK?**

No. Requests to pay by Venmo, Zelle, PayPal or gift card, to send extra money for shipping after a purchase, or to move the conversation to text, email or Instagram are all warning signs. Payments made outside SidelineSwap are not protected. Keep every message and payment on SidelineSwap, and report the user.

### **Recent scams we've seen**

All of these are fake.

* An email saying your item sold, with a QR code or a "Download now" button to view the sale or collect payment.
* An email from a "buyer" with an order or invoice number and a link to complete the order or enter bank details.
* A message saying your item sold and asking you to set up or verify your account through a Google Doc, Google Form or Google Drive link.
* A message asking you to verify your card, confirm your account balance or add money to PayPal before a buyer can pay you.
* A message saying SidelineSwap is changing its payment method and you must re-enter your card.
* A message asking you to "verify you're human" by entering card details.
* A message from an account posing as SidelineSwap support saying your account is restricted, under review or suspended, with a link to fix it.
* A message or email saying you have won a SidelineSwap credit.
* A request for your Social Security number from anyone other than the tax form in your Seller Dashboard.
* A buyer or seller asking to pay by Venmo, Zelle or gift card, or asking for extra shipping money after the sale.
