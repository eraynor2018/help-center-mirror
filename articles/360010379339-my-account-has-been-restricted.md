# My Account has been Restricted

It's likely that your account was automatically restricted by our security bot because you were attempting to take communications/transactions off the platform. Keeping communications on platform is to *your* benefit. When communications stay on SidelineSwap, you're guaranteed SidelineSwap Protection.

Another reason that your account may be restricted is because our security bot determined that you were spamming users. These three events currently trigger restrictions on SidelineSwap:

* New Users (registered within 24 hrs), creates 5 swaps in 30 minutes
* Any User creates 20 swaps in 24 hrs
* Any User sends the same exact message 5 times in 24 hrs

Please adjust your message frequency and content accordingly.

Please contact SidelineSwap Support to have your account reactivated.
