# Sales & Promotions

Occasionally, SidelineSwap will run site-wide "sales.” During a sale, Sellers can opt-in to discount their items by a preset percentage (usually 10-15%). All items that are entered into the sale will be spotlighted across all of SidelineSwap's social media channels and will be featured on the SidelineSwap homepage for the duration of the sale. 

If you opt your items into the sale, the prices on your items will be automatically dropped by that percentage when the sale begins. If your items are not sold at the conclusion of the sale, your items will return to their original price. 

The "Make Offer" feature on these listings will be disabled for the duration of the sale. If you wish to accept an offer below your posted price, you will need to remove your item from the sale, or manually reduce the price of your item. 

**Joining a Sale**

For a few days leading up to the sale and during the sale, you can join the sale by opting your items into the sale. There are a few ways to do this:

1. On desktop, you can hover your mouse over an item in your locker and click “Join Sale.”
2. On mobile devices, visit the item page and click "Join Sale" at the top of the page.
