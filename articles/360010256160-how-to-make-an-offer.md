# How to Make an Offer

On SidelineSwap, you have the option to make an offer below (or above) a Seller’s asking price by clicking “Make Offer” on the item page. You will only be charged if the Seller accepts your offer. Once the offer is accepted, the item is yours. If a Seller does not respond to your offer within 24 hours, the offer expires.

![Untitled design (7).png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/9945823286684)![Screenshot 2023-08-09 231734.png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/9945828885276) 

After we collect payment via our secure payment platform, the Seller will be notified and we will provide them a prepaid shipping label with your address. You will also be provided with the tracking number affiliated with that shipping label.
