# How to Insure your Items

Sellers can now insure their shipments directly on SidelineSwap through [Seel Worry-Free Delivery](https://www.seel.com/faq/learn-more-about-seel-worry-free-delivery). Insuring your shipments guarantees that your sale is protected in the event of a lost or damaged package. If you need help filing a claim for your insured shipment, read [here](https://sidelineswap.zendesk.com/knowledge/editor/01H7APCQ5EM2W6HYH9VHZPTENH/en-us?brand_id=360000813580).

If you'd like to insure your item, simply look for the "Add Worry-Free Delivery" toggle in the "Shipping Options" when you create or edit an item:

![Frame_13.png](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/article_attachments/9097980312860)

Insurance costs 1% of the price of your item, so if you list something for $100, $1 will come out of your earnings from the sale for insurance. *You are not charged until you make a sale*.

Please note certain terms and conditions do apply:

* Only shipments that use a SidelineSwap label can be insured
* Seel Worry Free Delivery only covers items that are less than or equal to $5000 in value.
