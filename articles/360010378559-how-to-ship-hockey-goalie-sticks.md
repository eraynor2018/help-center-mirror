# How to Ship Hockey Goalie Sticks

[SidelineSwap](https://sidelineswap.com/shop/skiing/skis) is a great place to buy and sell hockey sticks, both new and used. If you're looking to sell your complete stick, we've got a few tips to help you ship them safely.

#### **Materials You'll Need**

* Quality packing tape
* Bubble wrap

#### **Packaging**

Lie the stick on the floor and tear a strip of bubble wrap that is a few inches longer than the stick. Roll the bubble wrap around the stick and secure with tape at the top of the shaft, the middle of the shaft and around the blade. Apply a second layer of wrap around the blade for extra protection.

For [goalie sticks](https://sidelineswap.com/shop/hockey/sticks/goalie-sticks/l10930), we recommend a second layer of bubble wrap from the top of the paddle to the end of the blade.

Bubble wrap can be purchased at your local post office, office supply store and even most dollar/discount stores.
