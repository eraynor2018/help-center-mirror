# How to export my sales history

If your account has exceeded $600 in lifetime earnings, you may export a CSV of your sales history directly from your [Seller Dashboard.](https://sidelineswap.com/selling)

Merely click the "Export" button at the top right of your "Transaction History" list and we'll send you a CSV report to the email address associated with your account.
