# How can I cash out to Venmo?

Yes, you can cash out to Venmo and receive your funds within minutes. There's no lengthy registration process. Simply select "Cash out with Venmo" from your [Cashout page](https://sidelineswap.com/selling/cashout) and then register your Venmo account with your phone number!

To receive funds from Venmo, your Venmo account needs to be verified. Learn more [here](https://help.venmo.com/hc/en-us/articles/221073067-Verifying-Your-Bank-Account).

*Please note, SidelineSwap cannot be held responsible if you cash out to the wrong Venmo account. Be sure you have a Venmo account connected to your phone number before submitting your cashout.*

#### I cashed out to the wrong Venmo account

Venmo cashouts arrive within minutes and cannot be canceled, reversed, or redirected by SidelineSwap once submitted. As noted above, SidelineSwap can't be held responsible for funds sent to the wrong Venmo account, so before submitting, make sure the Venmo account connected to your phone number is correct and verified. If money was sent to the wrong account, you'll need to recover it through PayPal/Venmo directly.

#### Who can cash out to Venmo?

Cashing out to Venmo is available to U.S.-based sellers only. If you're outside the U.S., cash out to a bank account instead. Your Venmo account must be verified and connected to your phone number before you cash out.
