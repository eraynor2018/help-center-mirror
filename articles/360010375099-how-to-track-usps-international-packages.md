# How to Track USPS International Packages

SidelineSwap defaults to the cheapest shipping option available to our Buyers. For international shipments originating in the United States, this often means USPS First Class International.

With USPS First Class International, once a package clears through customs in the U.S., the tracking may sometimes not update in the foreign country.

*Do not worry,* the package is still on its way to you!

If you have a question regarding the package's whereabouts, it's best to contact your country's postal service's customer service line.
