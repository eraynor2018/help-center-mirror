# What does fixed rate shipping mean?

SidelineSwap has set fixed-rate shipping costs for most major categories on the site. This means that regardless of where your buyer is located, they will pay the same amount in shipping. (Note: You must use our suggested weight and dimensions to be eligible for fixed rates.) So buyers located in California and New York will pay the same shipping cost for the same gear (at no added cost to you).

Previously, if a seller listed a hockey stick for $100 some buyers might be charged an additional $9 shipping and others might be charged $19. Now sellers can list their stick for a set price with [Free Shipping](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/articles/4410656215314-how-does-free-shipping-work-) and know that the buyer will always see the same price and you'll always be charged your standard fee plus the fixed rate shipping amount.

*Please note, fixed rate shipping only applies to items sold domestically (so a US seller to a US buyer or a Canadian seller to a Canadian buyer). SidelineSwap does not have fixed international rates.*
