# Oversized Packaging

Due to the restrictions of our shipping provider USPS, certain oversized packages can be costly to ship. This serves as an issue to the Buyer and Seller. If shipping costs are too high, it may deter the Buyer from purchasing an item.

We recommend that the "length plus girth" for a package does not exceed **108 inches** (274.32 cm). The USPS has a very particular way for measuring "length plus girth" - [you can read about it here](http://pe.usps.com/text/qsg300/Q201e.htm). Any item exceeding 108 inches is extremely costly to ship.  
   
 Packages Parcel with "length plus girth" above 130 inches (330.2cm) cannot be shipped by USPS.
