# How Bundling on SidelineSwap Works 

If you would like to purchase multiple items from a seller’s locker, contact the seller before making the purchases and request that the seller create one “bundled” listing. The seller can either edit one of the existing listings or create a new one altogether for the desired bundle.

As a part of this step, it’s important that the seller update the weight and dimensions of the bundled listing to reflect all items that will be included in the package. For example, if you want to purchase hockey skates and a helmet, the weight of the new listing has to reflect the weight of the hockey skates plus the weight of the helmet. Without this step, sellers run the risk of their items being rejected at the post office.
