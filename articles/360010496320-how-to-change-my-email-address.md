# How to change my email address

You may change your the email address connected to your account from the [My Settings](https://sidelineswap.com/settings/account) page. Just click "Change", add your new email address and await the pin sent to your device for verification.

On the iOS App, you can do the same by going to your "Account" tab and then "Settings". From your Settings, find "Account Settings" and click into the email address field to update that information.
