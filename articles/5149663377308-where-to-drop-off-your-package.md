# Where to drop off your package

Regardless of the shipping carrier, there are drop-off locations all around you. Please enter your zip code at the links below to find drop locations closest to you.

FedEx and UPS partner with a number of businesses including CVS, Walgreens, and Duane Reade to create more drop-off locations for their customers.

**USPS Labels**

Find a local USPS drop-off location [here](https://tools.usps.com/find-location.htm%C2%A0) or [schedule a free pickup](https://tools.usps.com/schedule-pickup-steps.htm).

**FedEx Labels**

Find a FedEx drop-off location [here](https://local.fedex.com/en-us%C2%A0).

*Please note if your label has a FedEx and a USPS tracking code, drop your package off at a FedEx location*.

**UPS Labels**

Find a UPS drop-off location [here](https://www.ups.com/dropoff?loc=en_US%C2%A0).

**Canada Post Labels**

Find a Canada Post drop-off location [here](https://www.canadapost-postescanada.ca/information/app/fdl/business/findDepositLocation?execution=e2s1%C2%A0).
