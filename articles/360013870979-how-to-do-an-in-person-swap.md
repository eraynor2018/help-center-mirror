# How to do an in-person swap

If a Buyer and Seller live near each other and both have agreed to meet up for a local exchange, SidelineSwap can accommodate these swaps. 

How does a Swap in person work? 

Please reach out to SidelineSwap Support **once the exchange has taken place**. At that point, we can mark the swap as "Delivered" for the Buyer to approve the swap and release the funds to the Seller. We can also reimburse the Buyer for the cost of shipping. 

To make the necessary accommodations, SidelineSwap Support needs to see explicit communication in the swap page from both parties agreeing to the in-person exchange and confirming that it has taken place before we can make the necessary accommodations.

***Please note, all communications and transactions must take place on SidelineSwap.** Keeping communications on SidelineSwap is to your benefit. When communications stay on SidelineSwap, you're guaranteed SidelineSwap Protection. Remember, **SidleineSwap reserves the right to remove any user who fails to comply with these community guidelines.***
