# How to Avoid Delayed Tracking Scans

It is crucial that you ask USPS, UPS, or Canada Post to scan the package into their system when you drop your package off. If a package isn't showing scanned into the respective shipping service's tracking system, then SidelineSwap's system will not update either.

A Buyer will have the option to cancel their sale after 12 days of no shipment. The swap will automatically cancel 21 days after no shipment.
