# How do I redeem a gift card?

Instructions and a redemption code for your gift card will be sent to you via email. Gift cards can be activated by visiting [sidelineswap.com/gift-cards](https://sidelineswap.com/gift-cards). Once redeemed, gift card credits never expire.

The amount of the gift card will be immediately added to your account balance and made available for use after it has been redeemed. The associated credit cannot be transferred to a different account once redeemed. For purchases exceeding the amount of credit associated with a gift card, the remaining balance must be paid via credit card or PayPal. 

Click [here](https://help.sidelineswap.com/hc/en-us/articles/360013147939-How-to-buy-with-site-credit) to learn how to apply a credit to your SidelineSwap purchase.
