# Can I use a different Offer Autopilot setting on one listing?

Yes. Your Offer Autopilot setting applies to all of your listings by default, but you can give any listing its own setting. That listing then keeps its own setting even if you later change your account setting.

## Change the setting on one listing

1. Open the listing and tap **Edit**.
2. Find **Offer Autopilot**.
3. Choose **Set a floor price**, **Firm price**, or **Let me decide**.
4. Save your changes.

The new setting also applies to any offers on that listing you haven't answered yet.

## Stop Autopilot on one listing

Choose **Let me decide** on that listing. Autopilot won't respond to offers on it, and you'll answer them yourself. This works even if Autopilot is on for the rest of your account.

You can also do this from your swap page.

## Change many listings at once

From **My Locker**, select the listings you want to update and choose **Offer Autopilot**. This replaces the setting on every listing you selected, including any listing set to **Let me decide**.

## Put listings back on your account setting

When you change your account setting, you can choose to apply it to your current listings. This removes the custom setting from your listings so they follow your account setting again.

Listings set to **Let me decide** keep that setting. Change them one at a time if you want Autopilot to answer offers on them.
